I had the occasion, as part of my Laidlaw scholarship, to perform a 9 weeks summer research intenrship at the EPFL Laboratory of Intelligent Systems (LIS) of proffessor Dario Floreano.
My supervisors, Benjamin Jarvis and Yang Chen, are working on drone swarms. During my internship, I looked at how we could use haptics to convey swarm state informations. For that, we use a dorsal actuator (LRA and VCA) layout .
